# Solar panels

- Too high temperatures reduces PV efficiency by 0.4-0.5% per degree C
- Dust can reduce PV output up to 60% (esp in deserts)
- Terrain factors (albedo and snow) present mixed effects on PV generation

# Wind Turbine

- Ice and snow can cause up to 50% power loss and add mechanical stress
- Blades can be eroded by heavy rains, coastal salt spray, dust, sand, etc... leading up to 27% performance loss 
- Lightining strikes poses risks
- Extreme temperature switches affects lubrication and battery perfs
